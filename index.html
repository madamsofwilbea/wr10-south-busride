Before you sign up
  1. Open to everyone, pre-registered riders first. Anyone may join, but Madams who pre-registered in the group chat have first claim on a seat. If that's you, sign up with the exact IG handle on the group chat list. Held seats stay yours until 20 October — after that, anything unclaimed opens to everyone.
  2. Please be certain before you sign up. This is a shared ride with a shared cost. Every person who backs out pushes the fare up for everyone still going, and that lands hardest on the Madams travelling on a tight budget. Check your schedule, then commit.
  3. Be ready for an early start. We may need to leave earlier than you'd expect — the admins especially, since there may be a fanzone before the show. The departure time will be announced in the group chat and here, so plan your morning around it.
  4. One extra stop may be added. Depending on how arrangements land, we may add a single additional pick-up and drop-off point. If it happens, we'll announce it. Just one, though — more stops means a slower trip for everybody.
  5. Group booking is for pre-registered riders only. If you pre-registered, you may pay for up to five riders at once — as long as every person in that booking pre-registered too. Otherwise each person signs up separately. Everyone needs their own name, IG handle and number.
  6. Your seat is only yours once we've verified your payment. Signing up doesn't hold a seat by itself. We check every payment, then email you — that email is your seat.
  7. No refunds. Once you've paid, the money goes straight into securing the bus, so we can't return it if your plans change.

Pre-registered riders are booked as round trip, since the fare was worked out that way. We have everyone's number, so we'll text riders directly with the day's details too.
